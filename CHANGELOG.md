# Changelog
## Unreleased
- **The Rift (hybrid world design)**: save screen is now 4 slots (each slot is one game on the Helicarrier). A new building next to the plaza, the Rift Hall, holds a navigation console: type any coordinates (numbers or words), two Tesla coils zap the tear in the dimension, it stabilises and you step into a small Portal-Knights-style realm (generation 4): a spawn island plus 5-6 floating islands covering all four biomes, stepping stones, two Hellgates, bounded by the abyss. The same coordinates always make the same realm. A dimensional tear on the spawn island brings you home. Each realm saves its own builds inside the save. Co-op guests see the sequence and follow the host.
- **New blocks**: stairs (placed facing the way you look, 16 materials) and five neon rails (cyan, magenta, orange, blue, green) that join up with neighbours like fences.
- **Sky**: the tentacle bands now rise out of the mist at the front and sink into it at the back, so the loop has no visible seam. The sky is drawn after the terrain (cheaper).
- **Music**: the 8-bit death metal is gone. The score is now Minecraft-style: one slow track at a time (felt piano, pads, bells, a whispering choir, a deep drone), then a long silence before the next. Eldritch flavour: phrygian / locrian / octatonic scales, detuned piano, FM bells, reversed swells, minor-second clusters, a heartbeat that quickens in the dungeons and low drums for bosses. Tracks are described in data/music.json and re-composed on every play.
- **Sky polish**: stars twinkle on their own clocks and are short streaks; the star layer and two bands of abyss tentacles are independent background loops that slide past from the front to the back at their own set speeds (`SKY_STAR_SPEED`, `SKY_TENT_SPEED` in 27-daynight.js), so the station feels like it is moving. The tentacles' own waving is unchanged.
- **The Helicarrier is its own map** (world generation 3, the default for new worlds): an octagonal interdimensional space station hurtling through an eldritch deep-space sky (spiral nebula, hyperspace streaks out of a rift ahead, a swirling abyss below with huge tentacles rising from the miasma). No day or night out there. A dense neon megacity covers it: avenues, a core plaza with a lift tower and lamp canopy, an octagonal elevated promenade with four rail arms, apartment blocks, dark megablocks with cantilevers, brick shops with neon signs, needle towers with L-number plates, refineries and markets with vertical signs, skybridges between lots, the Hellgate at the north front and the dock at the south. Old worlds keep their generation.
- **New blocks**: 16 city blocks (hull plate, concrete, bricks, tech wall, street, lit windows, lamp panel, neon colours, hazard, teal, gold, pipe) and **half blocks (slabs)** of every one of them, bottom and top. You walk up half-block steps automatically; aim at the top half of a wall or under a block to place a top slab.
- **Sky + gate-and-beam day/night**: no more smooth cycle. Day = two suns, night = four moons. A dimensional gate rips open on the horizon, the other group climbs out, charges and fires beams that destroy the group that was there. Co-op follows the host's clock. The floating-island sky shows distant floating islands with lava falls, eldritch tentacles rising out of the horizon miasma, and a swirling abyss below.
- **Lava physics** (Minecraft rules): sources spread 3 blocks and thin out, lava with air under it falls, lava fills holes you dig, flows dry up when the source is removed. Host simulates, guests get one batched message per tick. Lava meshing: smooth shared corner heights (no cracks or steps), walls keep their faces next to lava, lowered lava shows under ceilings, diagonal chunk seams, wobble only on the surface, texture projected correctly on vertical faces.
- **The Helicarrier + floating islands** (world generation 2): new worlds are a sky of floating biome islands over an eldritch abyss, with a huge Helicarrier carrying a neon eldritch city, the Hellgate at its far front, and lava falls pouring from underside vents. Older worlds keep the old terrain and village (`meta.gen`).
- **Gamepad**: BACK opens the backpack / character screen, START opens the game menu; D-pad or left stick navigates every menu (A select, B back, left/right on sliders) and the backpack (A pick up/drop, X half/one, Y quick-move, LB/RB weapon, RT sort).
- **Menus**: Minecraft-style title screen with a living panorama, splash text, world list (create with name / seed / mode, rename, delete), options (volumes, render distance, FOV, sensitivity, shake, invert), pause menu, save and quit. Save schema 3: many worlds, each with its own blocks, time and inventory.
- **Sky**: sun and moons now set behind the horizon; two eldritch suns and four eldritch moons; fog only hides the render-distance edge.
- **Lava** flows (world-space animated shader, swelling surface, embers). **Item drops** from broken blocks. **Player model** (black skeleton-robot) and a character screen with loadout, equipment slots and backpack.
- **Textures** on every block, entity and weapon (procedural 16x16 atlas). **Day/night cycle** with a real sky (sun, eye-moon, stars), a clear horizon, night lighting and a clock in the HUD.
- **Open world**: endless streamed chunks, four eldritch-cyberpunk biomes, caves, ore, wild Hellgates into dungeon runs, fixed Ember Village at spawn. Save schema 2 (migrates old village builds). See docs/WORLD.md.
- **Inventory**: 36 slots, pick up what you dig, place from the hotbar, shards from demon ore sell to Vex, creative toggle.
- Co-op relay fallback through public MQTT brokers when two networks can't connect directly; connection test button; version shown in the CO-OP box.
- Co-op fixes: door seals sync (no walking through), rooms/chests/key/glyph respond to every player, shared "enemies left" counter, shots/slashes/sparks/sounds visible to all players.
- Co-op multiplayer (host + guests): CO-OP button, WebRTC join codes, shared hub & dungeon, shared enemies/bosses, damage both ways. See docs/MULTIPLAYER.md for what is still host-only.
- Enemies, bosses and hazards now target and hurt any player (`players[]`).
